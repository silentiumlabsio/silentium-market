# Public Glossary

This glossary explains SilentiumMarket terminology at a user-facing level. It does not document implementation details, formulas, thresholds, or private data structures.

## Command center

The desktop surface used for broader market review, planning context, historical outcomes, and public-facing diagnostics.

## False-positive suppression

The product principle that an attractive-looking market condition should not become an actionable recommendation until it survives independent quality and risk checks.

## GE Workstation

The RuneLite-side decision-support surface focused on the player's current Grand Exchange session, occupied and available slots, observed offer state, and manual next actions.

## Manual execution

The boundary requiring the player to perform Grand Exchange actions. SilentiumMarket provides analysis and guidance; it does not click, type, submit, cancel, or edit offers for the player.

## Market discovery

The early identification of items or conditions that may deserve further observation. Discovery alone is not a buy recommendation.

## Market quality

A high-level assessment of whether current observations appear sufficiently reliable and executable for decision support. Displayed spread alone is not treated as proof of opportunity.

## MAX DAY

A planning profile oriented toward shorter execution horizons and faster capital recycling for players who expect to revisit the Grand Exchange during the day.

## MAX NIGHT

A planning profile oriented toward durable multi-hour execution and later re-evaluation rather than indefinite holding.

## MAX SMART

A general risk-aware planning profile that considers available capital, open Grand Exchange capacity, uncertainty, and current market conditions.

## Personal Edge

Local analysis of a user's observed fills and execution outcomes. Personal history can inform estimates but does not replace current-market evidence.

## Profit-confidence estimate

A conservative presentation of uncertainty around an opportunity. It is decision support, not a promise that a trade will fill or produce profit.

## Profit Tracker

The user-facing record of verified merch outcomes, kept separate from unrelated Grand Exchange activity such as gear, supplies, or quest purchases.

## Replay and calibration

The review of historical recommendations against later market behavior to evaluate whether earlier observations remained executable.

## Validation

The evaluation stage between early discovery and an actionable plan. A discovered item may remain watch-only or be rejected when present conditions are not strong enough.

## Watchlist

A collection of items selected for monitoring or inspection. Inclusion does not imply a recommendation.
