# Known Limitations

SilentiumMarket is an advisory market-intelligence project operating in a changing live market. These limitations should be considered whenever its output is reviewed.

## No guaranteed outcome

No recommendation, plan, range, confidence presentation, or historical result guarantees that an offer will fill or that a trade will be profitable.

## Market conditions can change

Prices, liquidity, competition, and player demand can change after observations are collected or a plan is generated. A previously reasonable opportunity can become unsuitable before execution.

## Displayed spread is not executable profit

Visible buy and sell prices do not prove that sufficient volume is available or that both sides of a trade can be completed at those prices.

## Partial and delayed fills

Grand Exchange offers may fill partially, slowly, or not at all. Capital and exchange capacity can remain committed longer than expected.

## Public data has limits

Public market observations may be delayed, incomplete, stale, one-sided, or internally inconsistent. SilentiumMarket is designed to expose and reject weak conditions, but it cannot eliminate data uncertainty.

## Personal history is limited evidence

Observed user outcomes can improve personal estimates, but historical execution does not guarantee similar future behavior and does not replace current-market evidence.

## Classification may require review

Separating merch activity from gear, supplies, quest items, and other personal purchases can require manual confirmation or correction.

## Manual action remains required

SilentiumMarket does not perform Grand Exchange actions. The player remains responsible for reviewing guidance, entering offers, managing risk, and following applicable rules.

## Private testing status

SilentiumMarket remains under private testing. Public documentation can describe product direction and limitations, but it is not an installation or distribution channel.

## Independent project

SilentiumMarket is not affiliated with or endorsed by Jagex or RuneLite. Compatibility and external-service behavior may change outside Silentium Labs' control.
