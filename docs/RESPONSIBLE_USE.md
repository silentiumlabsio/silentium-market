# Responsible Use

SilentiumMarket is intended as a market-analysis and decision-support project.

## Manual game actions

Grand Exchange actions remain manual. The project is not intended to click, type, submit, cancel, edit, or otherwise perform RuneScape actions on behalf of the player.

## Market uncertainty

A recommendation is not a guarantee. Price, liquidity, and fill conditions can change after a plan is generated.

## Conservative interpretation

Users should treat buy ceilings, sell floors, confidence ranges, and review states as decision-support boundaries rather than promises of execution.

## Personal responsibility

Users remain responsible for their own RuneScape account activity and for following the rules of the services they use.
