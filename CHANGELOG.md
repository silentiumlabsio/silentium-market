# Public Changelog

This changelog intentionally summarizes user-visible progress without disclosing proprietary implementation details.

## v10.3.1

- Hardened market-quality and data-reliability checks.
- Improved rejection of stale, one-sided, and internally inconsistent market conditions.
- Added progressive candidate validation so MAX modes can search more broadly without weakening safety standards.
- Improved strategy-aware handling of active GE offers.
- Expanded plan diagnostics so rejected opportunities can be understood without being presented as buy calls.
- Improved local request efficiency and reliability telemetry.
- Refined portfolio sizing to account for market trust and execution uncertainty.
- Preserved the manual-execution boundary and local-only personal analytics model.

## v10.x foundation

The v10 generation established the current product direction:

- GE-first execution workstation.
- SMART, DAY, and NIGHT capital-allocation workflows.
- Conservative profit ranges and uncertainty-aware ranking.
- Personal execution learning and merch-only profit classification.
- Replay/calibration of historical recommendations.
- Explicit separation between early discovery and actionable recommendations.
